How I lead

Conviction at altitude. Warmth up close.

A leadership style built on informed point of view, operational rigor, and genuine care — refined over a decade and a half of doing the work.

Altitude & details

Lead at altitude, deliver in the details

Systems-level thinking and executive-legible communication — paired with a willingness to jump back into the file when the team is in crunch. Strategy and craft aren't separate jobs.

“The conductor of an orchestra — bringing clarity, harmony, and momentum to an incredibly complex effort, with limited resources.”

Safety

Psychological safety isn't soft

People do their best work when they can ask the dumb question, disagree, and be vulnerable. That environment is something you build on purpose — and defend.

“His openness and honesty create a safe environment. I always feel like he has my (our) back(s).”

Conviction

Conviction over consensus

Forms a real point of view from research and customer truth, then advocates for it — while staying genuinely open to being moved by a better argument.

“Adam has evolved from playing it safe to confidently pushing the boundaries in design — a clear positive impact on our solutions.”

Levity

Humor is a leadership tool

Used well, humor disarms tension and builds trust faster than any process. Calibrated to the moment — not suppressed, not performed.

“He brings a degree of levity to tough convos… This quality is frankly rare — but essential — in our line of work.”
